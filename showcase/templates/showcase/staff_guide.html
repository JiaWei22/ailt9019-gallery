Administrator guide

Use the independent AILT9019 Gallery staff account. These operations belong to the Gallery; do not change W5 rosters, credentials, tasks or historical records.

1. Configure the activity

Choose an activity in Staff → Configure event. Leave unknown dates and links blank; the activity page displays To be announced. Optional links must use HTTPS. Set the contact, intra-team rating form/deadline, Closing time/link, agenda and Moodle link when confirmed.

Before opening reviews/voting, set all four window boundaries and confirm the rules. Student Gallery submissions are disabled; Moodle is the submission channel. Leave both openings disabled while dates are unknown. Current course rules allow self-voting, ballot edits and review edits before cutoff. Restrict participation using existing Reviewer IDs; this does not modify W5 identities.

2. Import Moodle materials and publish

Collect each team's submission_links.txt from Moodle. Prepare a CSV using project_import_template.csv: youtube_url is the Presentation link; demo_youtube_url is the separate Demo link. Include the existing team_key, title, description, project_url and repository_url. Preview the import, fix row errors, then confirm. New projects are drafts: check both videos independently, then publish. Existing projects can be updated by project_id. Do not replace the roster.

Gallery status means Awaiting staff import, Awaiting staff publication or Published in Gallery; it never proves Moodle submission. Historical student receipts are retained for audit only. Students cannot publish even if legacy submission switches are enabled.

3. Assign and monitor reviews

Use Preview missing assignments, inspect participant/project counts and errors, then Confirm additions. Participants need three different published projects from other teams. If candidates are insufficient, no partial plan is applied. Monitor completion and export records as needed.

4. Withdraw and replace

Review publication change shows affected tasks, saved reviews and votes. Confirm withdrawal only after reviewing that impact. All related tasks, including completed reviews, are archived. Run Preview missing assignments and confirm replacements. Students must complete replacements. Republishing does not reactivate old tasks.

5. Count votes

Award-eligible totals and the CSV votes column exclude withdrawn projects and votes predating their latest withdrawal. recorded_votes counts choices in currently saved ballots; excluded_votes shows those excluded. They are not cumulative counts of every historic ballot revision. Republishing does not restore old votes; a student must resubmit a ballot within the voting window.

6. Close and export

Close event stops reviews and voting; student submissions are handled on Moodle. Freeze content separately when required. Review-detail exports retain historical reviews. Use counts_toward_completion to select current eligible reviews; assignment_active, project_published and reviewer_eligible explain exclusions. Export completion, review details, award totals and submission receipts; keep exports within the authorized course team. Audit history records changes. Never reset or delete history to fix a mistake.

Operational boundary

Backups, schema updates and service restarts are separate release operations, not dashboard actions. Follow the reviewed deployment/rollback plan; do not overwrite an active database with an old backup.
