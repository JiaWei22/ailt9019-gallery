Rules

Project submissions publish directly without administrator approval. Complete three assigned cross-team reviews. If a project is withdrawn, its completed review is retained but a replacement must be completed.

The confirmed course rules allow voting for your own team and editing ballots/reviews before the deadline. Check the voting page for the current activity settings.

Votes for withdrawn projects are retained as records but do not count toward awards. Republishing does not restore old votes automatically; submit a new ballot within the voting window to vote for the project again.
