Review guide · 10 points

Judge only the project page and video. Review three assigned projects from other teams anonymously using your semester Reviewer ID.

  • User value / 2: Is the problem and benefit clear from the page/video?
  • Working system / 3: Does the demonstrated workflow appear complete and credible?
  • Evidence / 2: Are sources, evaluation and limitations visible?
  • Communication / 2: Are the page and video concise and understandable?
  • Constructive comment / 1: Provide one specific strength and one actionable improvement.

Weak comment: “Nice project, well done.”

Constructive example: “The question-generation run at 1:10 is credible. Improvement: show expected vs actual answers for your 20-question check so the evidence is visible on the page.”

The comment score entered below is a reviewer-entered value, not an automatic award of course participation credit. Staff determine credit under the course rubric; a non-empty comment alone does not prove quality.
