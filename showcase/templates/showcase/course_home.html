About the course

From an idea to a working system

Present your users and problem, explain your project pipeline and domain customisation, and support your results with real evaluation evidence. Document limitations, responsible AI use and individual contributions.

Build and evaluate

Provide complete code, installation and test instructions, and evidence of the core workflow.

Communicate clearly

Share and review

Frequently asked questions

Where do I submit my videos?

Submit two separate YouTube links in submission_links.txt on Moodle. Do not upload video files to Gallery. Staff publish the links for you.

Why is my project not in Gallery yet?

Staff import and publish projects after collecting Moodle submissions. Gallery visibility does not confirm your Moodle submission status.

Do the two videos receive separate votes?

No. Votes are for the project as a whole. Watch both videos before reviewing or voting.

What access should my materials have?

Your project webpage must be public. The repository and both YouTube links must be accessible to the teaching team; video embedding must also work for Gallery viewers.

How do I enter the Gallery?

Use Gallery in the navigation and sign in with your existing Reviewer ID and password. Contact the course team if credentials or assignments are missing.
