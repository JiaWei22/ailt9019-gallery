Student guide

Activity dates and course links

1. Sign in

Use your existing semester Reviewer ID and password. For missing credentials or incorrect team membership, contact the course team. Do not share credentials.

2. Submit through Moodle

Submit your report and links file on Moodle, including separate Presentation and Demo YouTube links. Course staff import, check and publish projects in Gallery. There is no student publication form. Check Moodle for the official submission receipt; contact staff through Ed to correct Gallery materials.

{% include 'showcase/project_guidance.html' %}

3. Complete cross-team reviews

Open My reviews. Read the page and watch both videos and scoring guide for each assigned project; provide a specific strength and actionable improvement. Submit and check the saved status. You may edit before the review deadline. Archived tasks do not count toward the three active reviews; complete the replacement. If fewer than three are available, contact staff.

4. Vote

Select one to three distinct projects, including your own team if desired, review the ballot and save it. Saving again replaces your previous choices; it does not give additional votes. Changes stop at the voting deadline. Withdrawn projects' old votes do not count toward awards.

5. Finish course activities

Use the activity page for the confidential intra-team rating and Closing links once announced. Complete the required Moodle submission separately and check the group receipt there.

Problems

If a video fails, try Open on YouTube and report the project number to staff. If dates or links say To be announced, check the course announcements. A Gallery receipt confirms saved content, not video accessibility or Moodle submission.
